# SEO Change Log

One row per change. Log the change, why, and what you expect — then come back on the check date and record the result.

Columns:
- **Date** — when the change shipped.
- **Page/Template** — URL or template file touched.
- **Change** — what was done.
- **Problem targeted** — the issue it addresses.
- **Hypothesis** — expected effect.
- **Metric watched** — the GSC/GA4 metric that proves or disproves it.
- **Check date** — when to revisit (allow for recrawl; usually 2–4 weeks).
- **Result** — filled in on the check date.
